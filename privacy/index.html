Privacy

A short note on what the app uses. This is not a legal document and is not legal advice.

Lists

Have-lists and want-lists are saved on this phone. There is no account and no password. Your photo is saved only on this phone and is never shared.

To avoid repeat pings, this phone saves a short code for each of your last 80 matches: the other person’s random TableTrade ID, the kind of match, and the card IDs that matched. It does not save their name, note, or photo. Tap Hear pings again on Nearby to clear these codes, or clear TableTrade’s data. People you hide with Don’t show this person stay hidden until you clear TableTrade’s data.

Camera

The camera is for scanning one Pokémon card. The feed is for that moment. It is not stored as the product.

Bluetooth

While you are looking, your phone offers your name, your I’m over here note, and your card IDs over encrypted Bluetooth to phones nearby (usually the same room, sometimes a little farther). A device that only listens cannot read it. Any phone running TableTrade nearby can, because that is how matching works, and so could a tool built to talk like TableTrade. The Bluetooth signal itself still shows that a TableTrade phone is nearby. Keep Bluetooth on while you look. Closing TableTrade also stops it. Switching to another app may not.

Fonts

The app loads its fonts from Google when it starts, which sends your phone’s IP address. No lists or photos.

Backup

Android cloud backup is off for this app. On some phones, a direct phone-to-phone transfer may still copy TableTrade’s data to a new phone.

Age

You must be 13 or older to use TableTrade.
