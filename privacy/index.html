Privacy

A short note on what the app uses. This is not a legal document and is not legal advice.

Last updated 17 November 2026.

Lists

Have-lists and want-lists stay on this phone until you press Check in. There is no account and no password. Nothing from those lists is sent to a Range Road Technologies server.

To skip a repeat ping, this phone keeps the last 80 match IDs, and the IDs of people you hid with Don’t show this person. Names are not stored with them. Hear pings again (on Nearby, once a ping is on screen) clears the match IDs. Clear data clears the match IDs and the hidden people. Both stay on this phone only.

Camera

The camera is used on this phone only, to scan a card. The image is not stored and is not shared.

Bluetooth

When you press Check in, your phone shares this over Bluetooth with phones nearby: your display name (up to 24 characters), an optional note for how to find you (up to 40 characters), up to 12 card IDs from your have list and 12 from your want list, a random ID created for this install, and the time. Tap Done on Nearby to stop sharing. Closing TableTrade also stops it. Switching to another app may not.

Card search

Card search sends the name you type, or the name read from a card on the phone, to the Pokémon TCG API at api.pokemontcg.io. Card pictures load from images.pokemontcg.io. If that is down, the app searches TCGdex at api.tcgdex.net and loads card pictures from assets.tcgdex.net. Only the search text is sent, never your name or card lists. That is their service, not ours. We cannot delete their logs. The scan tool may download its language files from cdn.jsdelivr.net and tessdata.projectnaptha.com. Your lists are not uploaded there.

Backup

Android cloud backup is off for this app. On some phones, a direct phone-to-phone transfer may still copy TableTrade’s data to a new phone.

Age

You must be 13 or older to use TableTrade.
