Privacy

A short note on what the app uses in the shop. This is not a legal document and is not legal advice.

Lists stay on the phone

Have-lists and want-lists live on the device. There is no account and no password. When you check in at a shop, your display name and the cards on those lists are what get matched with people who also checked in there.

Camera

The camera is for scanning one Pokémon card, or an optional table QR. The feed is for that moment. It is not stored as the product.

Location

“I’m here” is optional. Location can hint which shop to pick. It does not prove you are in the room. A table code or QR is an optional extra join. Location is not stored as the product.

Unofficial

TableTrade is an unofficial fan tool. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Nintendo or The Pokémon Company.
