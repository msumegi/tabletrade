Privacy

A short note on what the app uses. This is not a legal document and is not legal advice.

Lists

Have-lists and want-lists live on the device until you tap I’m looking. There is no account and no password. While you are looking, your display name and those cards go over Bluetooth to other phones close by so they can ping. Face photos stay on this phone. This phone does not keep a history of who you met.

Camera

The camera is for scanning one Pokémon card. The feed is for that moment. It is not stored as the product.

Bluetooth

I’m looking uses Bluetooth to find phones in the same room. Keep Bluetooth on while you look. Keep the app open.

Age

You must be 13 or older to use TableTrade.
