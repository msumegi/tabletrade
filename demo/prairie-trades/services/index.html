Services

Work we will book.

If yours is not on the list, call. The answer is yes or no.

Plumbing

Supply, drains, fixtures.

Burst lines, slow drains, and new baths in houses plumbed when the yard was still pasture. The trucks carry couplings for copper, PEX, and the galvanized that still shows up.

Water running where it should not: shut the house valve. On a well, shut the pump breaker and call.

Heating

Furnaces, boilers, gas.

No-heat calls go first from November through March. Forced air, boilers, propane on the acreages, natural gas in town.

New equipment is quoted with the venting and the gas line, not as a box on a pad.

Hot water

Tanks, tankless, taps.

A tired tank usually announces itself with rust or a puddle. We will say if a repair is honest.

Tankless needs the gas supply and the vent sized properly. Both are checked before anyone picks a unit.

Heat pumps & cooling

Cold country. A few hot weeks.

A heat pump for most of the year, with the furnace as the deep-cold backup. Or air conditioning on a furnace still worth keeping.

Sized for the house you have, including the addition that was never on the drawing.

Acreage water

Tanks, pumps, yard hydrants.

Pressure tanks that have gone waterlogged, and hydrants that split in April. We do not drill wells. If the problem is the well itself, we say so.

A photo of the control box is enough to start.
