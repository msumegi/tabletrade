The regular loop

The loop we drive.

Benchland and the rural roads between six communities. If you are past the edge, call — the answer is either yes, with a travel note, or a straight no.

From the shop

Six communities. One shop.

Drive times are typical, in clear weather, from 14 Elevator Road. Between towns, give us the land description when you call.

The sketch shows the loop. The list is what we book against.

  • Benchland — the shop14 Elevator Road. Same-day for most in-town calls. This is home base.
  • Mile 18 — about 15 minutesAcreages and a handful of farmyards west of the shop. Gates, dogs, and long laneways.
  • North Coulee — about 20 minutesThe hall, the agriplex, and the houses gathered around them.
  • Wheatline — about 25 minutesNorth of the correction line. Tell us which side of the tracks.
  • Aspen Ferry — about 30 minutesAcross the bridge. In spring breakup, call before you assume the road is the road.
  • Rosebend — about 35 minutesThe far edge of the regular loop. Travel is still included. Past Rosebend, we price the extra miles first.

What to have ready

So the first call is useful.

Community or legal land description. Propane or natural gas. Whether anyone is without heat or drinking water. A photo of the equipment tag if the call is about a furnace, pump, or heater.

If the driveway needs a truck, not a car, say that too. We would rather know about the ruts before we commit the low van.
