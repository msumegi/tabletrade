02 — The agriplex

Footing for clinics. Boards for winter.

An 80 by 180 foot floor, seating for 220, and a seasonal outdoor rink when the weather agrees. Riding practice, stock shows, and the kids’ skate all live on the same grounds.

The fair

First weekend of September. Every year.

The 2026 fair has wrapped. The 2027 dates are set at the annual general meeting in January. Grounds, hall, and arena all turn over for two days.
