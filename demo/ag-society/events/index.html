September 2026

Fair weekend

Gates at 9. Hall, arena, and the grass between them. The 2026 fair is part of the sample year.

Sun 6 Sep · Grounds

Fair weekend, second day

Livestock show in the morning. Supper leftovers in the hall if the kitchen committee says so.

Sat 12 Sep · Agriplex

Fall riding derby

Practice at 9, first class at 10. Spectators in the stands. Example event.

Sat 19 Sep · Hall

Private booking

The hall is spoken for. No names — it is just a blocked day on a demo calendar.

Sat 26 Sep · Hall

Community potluck

Bring a dish that travels. Plates from 6. The meeting room stays open for the kids’ table.

October 2026

Sat 3 Oct · Agriplex

Arena footing work bee

Rakes, a tractor, and coffee from the warming room. Members and anyone willing.

Sat 10 Oct · Agriplex

Youth horse club

Practice for the younger riders. Helmets. A parent in the stands.

Sat 17 Oct · Hall

Harvest supper

The earlier of the two big suppers. Tickets at the door in this story, members first.

Sat 24 Oct · Hall

Private booking

Hall held. The arena stays free for evening practice.

Sat 31 Oct · Grounds

Community bonfire

West of the parking lot, away from the hall siding. Hot chocolate, not a show.

November 2026

Sat 7 Nov · Hall · 5:30 pm

Fall supper and pie auction

Doors at 5. Supper at 5:30. Pies go to the highest nod. Bring a pie if you can spare one.

Sun 8 Nov · Agriplex

Open ride

Member hour. Non-members pay the public rate. Footing was worked the weekend before, in the story.

Sat 14 Nov · Meeting room

Youth beef club

Club night in the meeting room. The main hall stays dark.

Sun 15 Nov · Agriplex

Gymkhana practice

Barrels and poles. Not a show. Spectators welcome in the stands.

Sat 21 Nov · Hall · 8 pm

Community dance

Local players. Lunch at midnight. Floor cleared by 1. Example evening.

Sat 28 Nov · Hall · 10–3

Craft and bake sale

Tables down the hall. Kitchen for the coffee. Vendors are neighbours in this fiction, not brands.

December 2026

Sat 5 Dec · Hall · 7 pm

Christmas concert

School and community numbers. Doors at 6:30. Members sit free in this telling.

Sun 6 Dec · Hall

Private booking

A held day. The calendar shows the block without inventing a host.

Sat 12 Dec · Outdoor rink

Kids’ skate and hot chocolate

Weather permitting. Chocolate in the warming room. Helmets for anyone under 12.

Sat 19 Dec · Hall

Carol sing and potluck

Song sheets on the chairs. Bring something warm in a pot you can carry.

Thu 31 Dec · Hall

New Year’s lease

Private. Music down at midnight is still the house rule.

January 2027

Sun 10 Jan · Meeting room

Board meeting

Open to members. The agenda is the fair, the rink, and the AGM packet.

Sat 16 Jan · Hall · 1 pm

Annual general meeting

Members in good standing vote. Memberships can be paid at the door. Example meeting.

Sat 24 Jan · Agriplex

Winter riding clinic

A clinician from inside the district, in the story. Limited riders. Spectators free.

Sat 31 Jan · Outdoor rink

Family skate

If the ice is in. If it is not, the warming room still has coffee and the date moves.

February 2027

Sat 6 Feb · Hall

Membership drive

Cards for the year, coffee, and a look at the arena schedule. Example fees on the membership page.

Sun 14 Feb · Hall

Sweetheart supper

A short sitting. Not a dance. Tickets from the hall desk in the week before.

Sat 20 Feb · Hall

Youth public speaking

The meeting room if the crowd is small, the main hall if it is not. Families welcome.

Sun 28 Feb · Agriplex

Arena work bee

End-of-winter tidy. Footing, boards, and the light that has been out since January.
