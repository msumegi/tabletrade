Community hall

Supper, dance, meeting, wedding lunch.

About 72 by 40 feet. Banquet for 180, standing room for 250, a meeting room for 28. The kitchen comes with a full day, and can be added to an evening.

Accessible entrance and washroom. A low stage on the west wall. Coat room by the door.

Arena

Footing, a booth, a winter rink.

80 by 180 feet of sand and dirt. Spectator seating for 220. Warming room, announcer booth, washrooms.

The outdoor rink is boarded and lit when the season allows. A mild December is not a promise.
