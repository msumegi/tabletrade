What comes with a booking

Tables & chairs

Hall rentals include the banquet tables, chairs, and the stage. You set them. A work bee can be arranged for society events.

A walkthrough

Keys from the hall desk on weekdays, or from the member on the key list the evening before. You walk the building together at the end.

What we ask

No confetti. Music down at midnight. Garbage in the bins by the north door. The kitchen as clean as you found it.
