The society

A card, a job, a vote.

Fees, the board, and the annual general meeting. A card is how you vote, and how you get the member rate.

Fees for the year

Pay at the desk, or at the door on AGM day.

Youth$10

Under 18. Speaks at the youth night. Does not vote.

Individual$25

One vote. Member rate on the hall and the arena.

Family$40

The household. Two votes. Same member rate.

Volunteer

Four jobs that keep the grounds open. Put your name down and someone from the board will call you back.

Hall setup

Event kitchen

Grounds

Grass, the rink boards, and the parking lot after the fair.
