TableTrade

Pokémon card trades close by.

TableTrade is the app for trading Pokémon cards with people near you. You keep a have-list and a want-list on your phone. Tap I’m looking. If someone close by is a match, both phones ping. Then you talk.

How it works

  1. Make your lists

    Add cards you’d trade and cards you want. Scan a card, or search a name and tap the printing.

  2. Check in

    Tap I’m looking. The phone uses location only to see who is close.

  3. Get the ping

    Photo, name, the cards, and an optional I’m over here line. Then you talk.

FAQ

Do I need an account?
No. Lists live on the phone. No password.
What is private?
Have-lists and want-lists stay on the device until you check in. Then your display name and those cards are what get matched. Camera is for scanning a card. Location is used while you are looking so matches close by can ping. It is not stored as a history. Short privacy note.
Is this official?
No. TableTrade is an unofficial fan tool. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Nintendo or The Pokémon Company.
