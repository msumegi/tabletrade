When you may need a business number

A business number (BN) is the standard identifier the CRA uses for a business or legal entity. Alberta’s Common Business Number page describes it as a federal 9-digit number.

Not every business must get a BN. The CRA says you need a BN if you need a GST/HST, payroll, or another CRA program account, or if you incorporate your business. If your business is unincorporated, you only need a BN when you register for program accounts with the CRA.

You can have only one BN for the same business or legal entity. New CRA program accounts are added to the BN you already have.

Registering with the CRA is not the only way to get a BN. The CRA says you may also receive one if you incorporate federally, or if you register or incorporate in certain provinces. Alberta is on that CRA list. Alberta partners with the CRA. After you form a corporation or non-profit organization, or register a business name, a federal business number is assigned. Alberta says there is no cost to obtain a business number.

When your information is entered at Corporate Registry, you receive proof of filing. Alberta says you will get an email when the federal business number is issued, unless one has already been assigned to you.

This course does not say whether you must register for GST/HST or payroll. Those are tax questions. Open the CRA’s “When you need a BN” page, and get help from the CRA or a qualified advisor if you are unsure.
