Use the official starting-a-business map

This course is a map. It shows official pages you can use if you want to start a small activity or micro-business in Canada, especially in Alberta. It does not say you will earn money. It is not legal, tax, or licensing advice. It does not replace a lawyer, an accountant, or a registry agent.

The Government of Canada's Starting a business page lists steps to consider before you operate a business:

  1. Planning a business
  2. Choosing a business name
  3. Registering your business with the government
  4. Applying for business permits and licences
  5. Getting business support and financing
  6. Free tax help for your business

You may not need every step on the first day. Selling a few items at a local market is not the same as hiring staff. The map helps you see which official page to open next.

This course walks through three parts of that map: how businesses are set up (the structure), when you may need a CRA business number, and where Alberta registries and licences live. For permits, you will also see BizPaL, a Government of Canada tool that lists permits and licences.

Start from the official pages, not from a social media ad or a paid “we will register you” site. Rules change. Check the live government page before you pay a fee or sign a form.

If you already keep invoices or GST records, that is a different skill. This course does not teach bookkeeping or tax filing.
