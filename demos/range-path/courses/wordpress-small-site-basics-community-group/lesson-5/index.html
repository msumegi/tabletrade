Help people find the page

A published page is not helpful if visitors cannot find it. WordPress.org says that after you create a page, you can add it to the site’s navigation menu.

On many sites you still open Appearance > Menus. WordPress.org’s steps are:

  1. Go to the Dashboard, then Appearance > Menus.
  2. Click Create a new menu, type a name, and click Create Menu.
  3. In the Pages pane, choose View All, tick the pages you want, and click Add to Menu.
  4. Drag items up or down to change the order. Drag an item slightly to the right to make it a sub-item.
  5. Under Menu Settings, choose a display location if your theme lists one, then click Save Menu.

Keep the menu simple so people are not confused. About, Events, and Contact are enough for many groups.

WordPress.org notes that WordPress 5.9 was the last version to include the Appearance Menus screen for all sites by default. On WordPress 6.0 and later, a block theme may manage navigation with a Navigation block instead. If you do not see Appearance > Menus, do not guess. Ask the person who set up the site, or read the current WordPress.org documentation for your theme.

This course does not recommend a theme or a plugin to “fix” menus. When the words are clear and the menu points to the right pages, your group’s small site is doing its job.
