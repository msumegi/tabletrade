Follow the official “how to earn” map

Alberta’s “How to earn your CAEC” page is the official map. The process can vary by province or territory, so use Alberta’s site if you live in Alberta.

Under Study, the page points you to official resources:

  • Review the test outcomes. These outline the knowledge and skills needed for each test.
  • Practise with a sample test, available in each subject.
  • Watch the official CAEC video to learn more about how tests are formatted, written, and scored.
  • Take a test preparation class, available at some testing centres.
  • Visit regional CAEC websites for more information.

This course does not replace those official tools. It does not include sample-test questions, and it does not claim that using a study plan will make you pass.

Under Register, the page tells you to check local eligibility on your provincial or territorial website, create an account on the CAEC digital platform, and schedule your test with a testing centre. You write tests in person at a testing centre on the date and time you selected. The page also says to provide proof of identification and to check your province or territory for fees. This course does not quote fees.

You can complete the CAEC at your own pace. The official page says there is no specific timeframe in which you need to write the tests, and the tests do not expire. That is good news if you work or care for family. You can plan one subject at a time.

If you have questions about older GED® results counting toward a CAEC, read the official how-to page. Do not use this course as the rule.
