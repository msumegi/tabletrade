What the CAEC is, and what this course is not

The Canadian Adult Education Credential (CAEC) is a Canadian adult education credential. Alberta’s official page says it is for adults who do not have a high school diploma and who are seeking employment prospects, education pathways, or training opportunities. You should confirm directly with employers, post-secondary institutions, and others that the CAEC meets their job or admission requirements.

This course is study-skills support. Finishing it does not mean you have earned the CAEC. It does not promise that you will pass any test. It is not a replacement for a tutor, a preparation class, or the official tests.

The official page says the CAEC replaced the GED® in 2024. Completed GED® credentials remain valid. As of 10 November 2026 (America/Regina), that page said they will continue to be recognized as a valid credential until December 2027. This course does not teach former GED® tests.

CAEC tests are available in these subject areas: Reading, Writing, Math, Science, and Social Studies. All tests are available in English and French. They are computer-based, with a paper option, and they are meant to support test-takers who need learning accommodations. The official page also lists how each test is set up. Those details can change, so read them on the live page instead of memorizing them here.

Start with Alberta’s CAEC page every time you have a question about the credential. Do not rely on an unofficial social media post or a paid “you will pass” website.
