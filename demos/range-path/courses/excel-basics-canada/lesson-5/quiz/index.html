Write a simple formula that starts with =

Choose an answer, then check it. Your score stays on this device.

Question 1 of 7

  1. Every Excel formula begins with:

    Formulas always begin with `=`.

  2. The operator for multiplication is:

    Asterisk means multiply.

  3. `=4/2` gives:

    4 divided by 2 is 2.

  4. A formula that adds Monday to Wednesday hours on the sample Hours sheet is:

    Start with `=` and point at the cells.

  5. Microsoft's example `=5+2*3` calculates as:

    Multiply first, then add; result 11.

  6. After a formula calculates, the formula bar shows:

    Select the cell and read the formula bar.

  7. If you type `2+4` without `=` and press Enter, Excel will often:

    Without `=`, Excel usually does not treat the entry as a formula.
