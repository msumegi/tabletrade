Save, print, and share without leaking private information

Choose an answer, then check it. Your score stays on this device.

Question 1 of 8

  1. After a workbook has been saved once, a quick way to save again in the same place is:

    Save or Ctrl+S.

  2. File > Save As is the Microsoft path to use when:

    First save, new location, or copy.

  3. AutoRecovery:

    AutoRecovery is not a substitute for saving.

  4. Before printing, Microsoft's basic-tasks page has you:

    Preview first.

  5. To export a PDF from desktop Excel, a Microsoft path is:

    File > Save As or Save a Copy > PDF.

  6. Sharing a PDF:

    Microsoft's PDF warning.

  7. Before you email a workbook, you should:

    Open every tab; do not rely on a filter to hide private rows.

  8. Get Cyber Safe advice applied here includes:

    Know why you are sharing, and share as little as you can.
