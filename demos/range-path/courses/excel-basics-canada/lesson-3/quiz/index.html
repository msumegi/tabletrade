Enter text, numbers, and dates

Choose an answer, then check it. Your score stays on this device.

Question 1 of 7

  1. After you click a cell and type, you finish by:

    Microsoft: press Enter or Tab to move to the next cell.

  2. Which entry belongs in an Hours worked column if you want Excel to add the numbers later?

    Keep the value as a number; put the unit in the header.

  3. On the sample Hours list, Thursday's `0` means:

    A typed 0 is recorded information.

  4. A good place for the letters CAD or the word hours is:

    Headers carry the unit (Hours worked, Amount (CAD)).

  5. To make a cell display as a date, a Microsoft path is:

    Home > General arrow > Date or More Number Formats.

  6. If a long note looks cut off in the grid:

    The formula bar shows the full cell contents.

  7. Date layouts in Excel:

    Microsoft notes that date formats depend on type and locale; some follow regional settings. We do not treat one layout as the only Canadian form.
