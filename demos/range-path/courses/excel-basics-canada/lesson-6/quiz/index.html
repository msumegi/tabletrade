AutoSum, AVERAGE, and checking that the answer makes sense

Choose an answer, then check it. Your score stays on this device.

Question 1 of 8

  1. To total a column with AutoSum, first select:

    Below a column (or to the right of a row).

  2. AutoSum writes a formula that uses the function:

    AutoSum inserts SUM.

  3. `=SUM(B2:B8)` means:

    A colon marks a range.

  4. A Microsoft path for Average is:

    Formulas > AutoSum arrow > Average.

  5. With sample hours 6, 4, 7.5, 0, 5, 3, 0, `=SUM(B2:B8)` is:

    6+4+7.5+0+5+3+0 = 25.5.

  6. Including two zero days in AVERAGE:

    Zeros increase the count of values and pull the average down.

  7. If AutoSum proposes a range that includes your total cell as well as the days:

    Check the highlight before you confirm.

  8. A useful sense-check for a total is:

    Estimate and read the formula — a Skills for Success style numeracy check.
