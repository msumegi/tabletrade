Share a file safely

You can share a Drive file or folder and choose what other people may do. Google’s sharing page describes three roles:

  • Viewer: can view the file or folder, but cannot make changes, add comments, or share the item
  • Commenter: can make comments and suggestions
  • Editor: can open, edit, delete, or move files in a folder and add new files. Editors can also change permissions and share with others by default

To share with specific people:

  1. Go to Google Drive, Docs, Sheets, or Slides.
  2. Select the item and click Share.
  3. Enter the person’s email address.
  4. Select Viewer, Commenter, or Editor.
  5. Choose whether to notify them, then click Send or Share.

General access can be Restricted (only people you have given access) or Anyone with the link (anyone on the internet who has the link can open it without signing in). If you use a work or school account, an administrator may restrict outside sharing.

Giving “Anyone with the link” access can spread a file farther than you expect. Do not use that setting for private information. When you share a folder, files inside it inherit those permissions.

If you no longer want someone to have access, open Share, find the person, and choose Remove access.

This course is not a security audit. When you are unsure, keep the file Restricted and share only with people you know.
