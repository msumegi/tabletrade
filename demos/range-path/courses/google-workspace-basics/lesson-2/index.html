Find your files in Google Drive

Google Drive helps you keep files together. Google’s Drive help says you can upload and share files, create new files, keep them organized, and open them from different devices.

On a computer:

  1. Go to drive.google.com.
  2. At the top left, click New.
  3. Choose what you need: New folder, Folder upload, a document type (to create a file), or File upload.

My Drive holds files and folders you upload or sync, and Google Docs, Sheets, Slides, and Forms you create. Files that other people shared with you appear in Shared with me.

The Home section can show files you or others recently opened, shared, or edited. You can search and use filters such as type, people, modified date, or location.

To see information about a file, click it and choose View details. That can include activity, sharing permissions, and recent changes.

To move a file you no longer need out of the way, Google says you can select it and choose Move to trash.

Do not upload someone else’s private documents for practise. Use a sample file you created, or a practise file your instructor gives you.

If you use a work or school account, your organization may set extra rules. Follow those rules.
