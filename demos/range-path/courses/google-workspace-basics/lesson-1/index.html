What is Google Workspace?

Google Workspace is a set of apps you can use in a web browser. Google’s Workspace Learning Center says you can start with apps such as Gmail, Docs, and Drive, and work from different devices.

The same Learning Center groups the apps by what they help you do:

  • Connect: Gmail (email), Calendar, Chat, and Meet (video and voice meetings)
  • Create: Docs (word processing), Sheets (spreadsheets), Slides (presentations), and Forms (surveys and quizzes)
  • Store and find files: Drive (cloud storage)

You do not need every app for this course. The next lessons practise Drive, Docs, Sheets, and sharing. A later Range Path course covers Microsoft Word, Excel, and PowerPoint if those are the tools at your site. This course does not replace those Microsoft courses, and it is not a full Gmail or Meet class.

If your council, library, or workplace gives you a work or school Google account, your administrator may turn some features on or off. If you use your own account, follow the sign-in they approve on that computer. This course will not create an account for you and will not ask you to type a password into the lesson.

Google’s help also notes accessibility options in the Learning Center. Ask your instructor if you need those tools.
