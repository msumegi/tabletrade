Reply, Reply All, Forward, and To / Cc / Bcc

Choose an answer, then check it. Your score stays on this device.

Question 1 of 7

  1. Reply sends your message to:

    Reply goes only to the original sender.

  2. Reply All includes:

    Reply All includes the sender plus To and Cc recipients.

  3. When you Reply or Reply All, original attachments are:

    Attachments are not included on Reply or Reply All.

  4. Forward is the better choice when you:

    Forward includes original attachments and a new recipient list.

  5. People who need to respond or take action belong on:

    Microsoft puts people who must act on To and people who must be informed on Cc.

  6. Microsoft says a careful way to show a manager a sensitive sent message is to:

    Forward after sending; a Bcc recipient might reply-all and reveal themselves.

  7. If you want to leave a large distribution list, Microsoft says you should:

    Never Reply All only to ask to be removed.
