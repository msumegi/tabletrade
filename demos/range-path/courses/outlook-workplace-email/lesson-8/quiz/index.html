Contacts, tasks, and good daily habits

Choose an answer, then check it. Your score stays on this device.

Question 1 of 7

  1. Microsoft says you find contacts under:

    Contacts live under People.

  2. To create a contact, Microsoft says you can:

    New Contact, or Ctrl+Shift+C from any folder.

  3. A basic contact for this course should include:

    A name and email are enough; never store passwords or SINs in a practice contact.

  4. Microsoft’s steps for a new task include:

    New Items > Task, then Save & Close; shortcut Ctrl+Shift+K.

  5. Assigned tasks to other people are:

    You can assign a task at work, but this course stays with a task you create for yourself.

  6. A calm daily habit in Mail is to:

    Read first, then reply with care.

  7. On a shared computer, when you finish your Outlook practice you should:

    Sign out on a shared computer.
