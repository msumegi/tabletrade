Spot junk and phishing without panicking

Question 1 of 7

  1. Microsoft says mail identified as possible junk can be:

    Microsoft says possible junk can go to the Junk Email folder and malware is disabled.

  2. Phishing is best described as:

    Phishing tries to trick you into clicking, opening a file, or giving away private details.

  3. Get Cyber Safe says that if you do not have an account with the organization named in the message, you should:

    Get Cyber Safe: if you have no account with that organization, delete the message.

  4. A mismatched or misspelled sender address, urgent threats, and a request not to use official contact details are:

    Those are Get Cyber Safe warning signs — pause and do not click.

  5. If you need to check whether a bank message is real, a careful step is to:

    Verify with official details you find yourself, not details supplied in the suspicious message.

  6. The Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre report page this course opened on 10 November 2026 points people to:

    reportcyberandfraud.canada.ca or 1-888-495-8501. Contact local police if you were a victim.

    This lesson points to the email-safety course for more depth.
