Calendar: appointments and a simple meeting

Choose an answer, then check it. Your score stays on this device.

Question 1 of 7

  1. Microsoft says an appointment is time on your calendar that:

    Appointments do not invite others or reserve resources.

  2. A meeting, in Microsoft’s terms, is time that:

    Meetings include other people and can include a room.

  3. To create an appointment, Microsoft says you can:

    New Appointment, or Ctrl+Shift+A. You can also right-click a time block.

  4. To create a meeting, Microsoft says you can:

    New Meeting, or Ctrl+Shift+Q from any folder.

  5. Meeting responses, Microsoft says, arrive:

    Replies to meeting requests arrive in the Inbox.

  6. A reminder on an appointment or meeting is set by:

    Use the Reminder list on the appointment or meeting.

  7. You want a private half hour to review notes and a separate fifteen minutes with Jordan. A careful plan is to:

    Keep your own review time as an appointment; invite Jordan with a meeting.
