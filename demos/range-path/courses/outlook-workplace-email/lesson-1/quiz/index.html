Question 1 of 7

  1. Microsoft Outlook is a program that organizes:

    Microsoft’s basic Outlook page says Outlook organizes emails, calendars, contacts, tasks, and to-do lists.

  2. The first time Outlook starts on a computer, Microsoft says setup may ask for:

    Auto Account Setup may ask for your name, email address, and a password. Do not type a real workplace password into this course.

  3. In this course you should:

    Use a tutor-approved practice account.

  4. To see your schedule you select:

    Calendar sits with Mail, People, and Tasks.

  5. New messages usually arrive in:

    Microsoft names the Inbox as a mail folder you can work from; new mail usually arrives there.

  6. Switching from Mail to Calendar:

    Switching views does not delete mail.

  7. If a label on your screen does not match this lesson, a careful next step is to:

    Screens differ across classic, new, web, and mobile Outlook.
