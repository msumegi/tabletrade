What Outlook is and how to start a message

Microsoft Outlook is a program that keeps email, calendars, contacts, and tasks in one place. Microsoft’s basic-tasks guide says that after you set up an account you can send and receive email, use the calendar, save contacts, and work with tasks.

The first time you start Outlook, it may ask for your name, email address, and password. That is often enough. If setup fails, Outlook may ask for more details, such as a mail server name, which your email provider can give you. You can add another account later from File > Add Account. Do not enter a real password into this course — practise only on an account your tutor or workplace has approved.

A quick access or navigation area lets you move among Mail, Calendar, People, and Tasks.

To write a new email (Microsoft):

  1. From a mail folder such as your Inbox, choose New Email. (A keyboard shortcut Microsoft lists is Ctrl+Shift+M.)
  2. Add recipients, a subject, and your message.
  3. Choose Send when you are done.

The Inbox is where new mail usually arrives. Click a message to read it in the reading pane or open it fully.

If Outlook is not on the computer in front of you, ask your adult learning council which version they have. This course is not MOS training and not a tech-support contract.
