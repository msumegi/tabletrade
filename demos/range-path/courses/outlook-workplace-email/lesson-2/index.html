Reply, Reply All, or Forward

When you receive a message, Microsoft lists three common actions:

  • Reply sends your new message only to the original sender. Attachments from the original message are not included.
  • Reply All sends your new message to the original sender and everyone on the original To and Cc lines. Attachments are not included.
  • Forward lets you send the message to new people. Attachments from the original message are included automatically.

You will find Reply, Reply All, and Forward on the ribbon, beside the message, or at the top of the reading pane, depending on your screen and Outlook version.

Microsoft asks you to think before you choose Reply All, especially if the message went to many people. It is often better to choose Reply and then add only the people who truly need your answer. If you do use Reply All, remove anyone who does not need the message.

After you choose an option:

  1. Write your message.
  2. Add or remove names in the To, Cc, or Bcc boxes if needed.
  3. Check the subject line. A reply usually starts with RE: and a forward with FW: — you can change the subject if you want.
  4. Select Send.

If you reply and later need to send a file, attach it yourself. If you need to pass the original files to someone new, Forward is the action that keeps those attachments.
