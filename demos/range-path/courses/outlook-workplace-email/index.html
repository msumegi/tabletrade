Microsoft Outlook and Workplace Email Habits (Canada)

By the end, learners can find Mail and Calendar, write a clear message, choose Reply or Reply All with care, spot a labelled phishing message, and add a simple appointment.

Syllabus

You do not need a password or a payment to open these lessons. Your place is saved on this device only.

About 16 hours (estimate, including practice and the exam). Each lesson’s reading time is shorter; the rest is practice.

What's included

a practice workbook, in-lesson checks, a practice task in each ready lesson, a cheat sheet you can print, a 70% final with 2 attempts, a sample certificate.

Print the course cheat sheet. You can also save that page as a PDF from the print dialog.

Final exam and certificate

The final exam covers all 8 lessons. The pass mark is 70%. You have 2 attempts. There is no timer. The certificate means you passed the knowledge exam. It does not mean a tutor watched you do the task. It stays locked until you pass, and it is not an accredited credential.
