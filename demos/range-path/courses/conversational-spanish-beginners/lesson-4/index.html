Numbers and useful everyday phrases

Numbers help in a shop, a market, or when you are counting items.

  • uno — one
  • dos — two
  • tres — three
  • cuatro — four
  • cinco — five
  • seis — six
  • siete — seven
  • ocho — eight
  • nueve — nine
  • diez — ten

Useful phrases:

  • ¿Cuánto cuesta? — How much does it cost?
  • ¿Dónde está…? — Where is…?
  • el baño — the washroom
  • el agua — water
  • ayuda — help
  • ¿Me puede ayudar, por favor? — Can you help me, please?

Example: ¿Dónde está el baño, por favor? Example: ¿Cuánto cuesta? (you can point to the item)

In Canada you will still see prices in dollars. You do not need to change the currency words to use ¿Cuánto cuesta? The other person can show you the price or say it in English.

Say the numbers out loud several times. Pair them with your fingers if that helps you remember. Skills for Success communication includes speaking with clarity — a slow, clear uno, dos, tres is better than a rushed guess.

This lesson is phrase practice, not a math course and not shopping or tax advice.
