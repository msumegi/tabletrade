Lesson 5 of 5

Put a short conversation together

Now join the pieces. Imagine you meet someone at a community hall or in a shop.

You: Buenos días. Me llamo Jordan. ¿Cómo está usted? Them: Bien, gracias. ¿Y usted? You: Bien, gracias. Mucho gusto. Them: Igualmente.

If you need something in the shop:

You: Hola. ¿Cuánto cuesta? ¿Me puede ayudar, por favor? If you get lost in the words: No entiendo. Más despacio, por favor. ¿Habla inglés?

Practise the whole exchange out loud two or three times. Record yourself on your phone if you like, then listen. Skills for Success communication includes both speaking with purpose and listening to understand.

Remember:

  • Spanish varies by country and region. A neighbour from Mexico, Spain, or Colombia may use a slightly different word. The greetings in this course are still a polite start.
  • Finishing this course does not make you fluent and is not a language credential.
  • Keep practising with a class, a tutor, a language-exchange partner, or short daily review. A real conversation class gives you listening practice this page cannot give.
  • Official languages in Canada are English and French. Learning a few Spanish phrases is extra help for daily life, not a replacement for English or French skills you may also be building.

Use the phrases you know. Be willing to say No entiendo. That is real communication.
