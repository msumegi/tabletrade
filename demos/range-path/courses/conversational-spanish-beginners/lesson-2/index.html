Courtesy words and introducing yourself

Courtesy words help a short conversation feel respectful.

  • Por favor — Please
  • Gracias — Thank you
  • De nada — You’re welcome
  • Perdón — Sorry, or “excuse me” if you bump into someone
  • Con permiso — Excuse me, when you need to pass by
  • Sí — Yes
  • No — No

To introduce yourself:

  • Me llamo [your name]. — My name is [your name].
  • ¿Cómo te llamas? — What is your name? (informal, with someone you know well)
  • ¿Cómo se llama usted? — What is your name? (more formal)
  • Mucho gusto. — Nice to meet you.
  • Igualmente. — Likewise.

Spanish has a more formal “you” (usted) and a more informal “you” (tú). With a customer, an older person, or someone you have just met, usted is often the safer, more respectful choice. With a close friend, tú is common. If you are not sure, start formal.

Try this out loud:

Hola. Me llamo Alex. ¿Cómo se llama usted? Mucho gusto.

Skills for Success communication includes adapting to your audience. A workplace or shop greeting is usually calmer and more formal than a chat with a close friend. Speak clearly. You do not need a perfect accent to be understood.
