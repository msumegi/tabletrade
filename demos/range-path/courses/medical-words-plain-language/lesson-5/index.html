Where to find trustworthy Canadian health information

When you look up a health word, start with official Canadian pages:

  • Health Canada and It’s Your Health articles on Canada.ca, for easy-to-understand health and safety information
  • Alberta.ca pages such as Know Your Options, AHCIP, and health cards, for how care works in Alberta
  • Primary Care Alberta and Alberta Find a Provider, for what primary care, family doctors, nurse practitioners, and walk-in clinics are
  • MyHealth Alberta official phone-number lists, including 911 and 811

These sources will not replace a conversation with a clinician. They can help you prepare questions and check that a word means what you think it means.

Be careful with:

  • social media tips that do not name an official source
  • sites from another country that may describe different drugs, doses, or systems (this course does not use those as source pages)
  • anyone, including this course, who claims to diagnose you from a word list

If you feel unwell and do not know where to go, call 811. If it is a medical emergency, call 911. If you need AHCIP or a health card, go back to the Alberta health-care basics course and the live Alberta.ca pages.

Finishing this course does not make you a nurse, medical office assistant, or billing specialist. It helps you ask clearer questions.
