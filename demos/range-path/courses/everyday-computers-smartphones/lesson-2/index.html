Lesson 2 of 5

Turn on, look around, and lock the screen

Before you learn programs, get comfortable with the device in front of you.

Start up. Press the power button until the screen lights up. Wait. The first screen may ask you to swipe, tap, or type a password, PIN, or use a fingerprint. Get Cyber Safe says to give a computer a strong log-in password and to lock it when you are not around. On a phone or tablet, use a lock-screen password and turn on extra lock options, such as a fingerprint, if they are available. Also turn on auto-lock so the device locks after a short time.

Look around.

  • On a computer, the mouse or trackpad moves a pointer. A single click usually selects something. A double-click often opens it.
  • On a phone or tablet, a light tap often opens an item. Swipe to move around the screen.
  • The keyboard (physical or on-screen) is for typing.

If you open the wrong thing, look for a Back arrow, an X to close a window, or the Home button or gesture on a phone. Skills for Success includes knowing when to use Back and Next, and how to scroll.

Lock when you step away. Even at home, locking the screen helps keep photos, messages, and files private. Do not write a password on a sticky note beside the device. Get Cyber Safe says never to write a password down and then store it near the computer, and never to share your password with anyone.

Practise turning the device on, unlocking it with help from a tutor if needed, opening one app, closing it, and locking the screen again.
