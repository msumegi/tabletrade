Power on, lock the screen, and find Settings

Choose an answer, then check it. Your score stays on this device.

Question 1 of 7

  1. A good habit when you leave a computer is to:

    Get Cyber Safe: lock when you are not around.

  2. On many Windows computers, an official lock shortcut is:

    Microsoft: Windows key + L, or Start > power > Lock.

  3. On Android, None and Swipe:

    Google: None and Swipe give no protection.

  4. Get Cyber Safe says auto-lock on a phone is useful because:

    Get Cyber Safe phones page: enable auto-lock.

  5. True or false: It is safe to keep your password on a note stuck to the screen.

    Do not store a written password near the computer.

  6. If you cannot find “Security” on an Android phone, Google says to:

    Google notes that steps vary; check the maker’s help.

  7. Signing out of Windows is different from locking because:

    Microsoft: sign out closes the session; lock keeps apps open.
