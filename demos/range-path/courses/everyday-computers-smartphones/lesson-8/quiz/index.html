Everyday email and messages on a phone or a computer

Choose an answer, then check it. Your score stays on this device.

Question 1 of 7

  1. Email is different from a text because it usually uses:

    Email uses an address and usually a subject.

  2. Gmail’s computer steps for a new message start with:

    Then To, subject, message, and Send (Gmail help).

  3. Get Cyber Safe says you should not use texts to:

    Get Cyber Safe: do not send personal or sensitive information by text.

  4. If a text says it is from your bank and includes a phone number you do not know, Get Cyber Safe says:

    Never call numbers in suspicious messages, even if they name a bank.

  5. A first practice email in this course should go to:

    Tutor or practice address only.

  6. True or false: It is fine to open any attachment that arrives by text.

    Never open attachments from unknown or untrusted sources.

  7. On a shared computer, a good habit after you use mail is to:

    Sign out so the next person does not use your mail.
