Email & Online Safety Basics in Canada

You need at least 5 out of 7. A pass opens a sample certificate on this device.

Question 1 of 7

  1. Best first move on a threatening “CRA gift card” call? Hang up and verify later on canada.ca.

    Refunds and account changes belong on official CRA channels, not surprise links.

  2. Official CRA sites start with canada.ca or end in cra-arc.gc.ca.

    Trust addresses that start with canada.ca or end in cra-arc.gc.ca.

  3. Phishing tries to trick you into giving information or clicking.

    That is what phishing is for.

  4. Reusing one password everywhere is safe.

    One breach can open many accounts. Use a unique password or passphrase.

  5. CAFC wants reports only after large losses.

    Report even if you did not lose money.

  6. A passphrase of four random words can be stronger than a short common password.

    Length and random words are the idea behind a passphrase.

  7. Clicking a surprise refund link is the recommended way to get CRA payments.

    Type canada.ca yourself. Do not use a surprise link.
