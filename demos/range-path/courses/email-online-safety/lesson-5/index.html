How to report fraud in Canada

If you spot a scam — or you already shared information — reporting helps you and your community.

1. Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre (CAFC)
Report online or call toll-free 1-888-495-8501. Report even if you did not lose money. Keep notes: date, phone numbers, emails, screenshots, and what they asked for.

2. Canada Revenue Agency (CRA)
If the scam involved your tax or benefits account, or you see unauthorized changes, use the CRA’s report-a-scam / identity theft options (online form or phone). They can help place protections on your account.

3. Local police
Especially important if you lost money. Ask for a file number and keep it.

4. Your bank or credit union
Call them quickly if you sent money or shared banking passwords. Ask about next steps and monitoring.

5. Credit bureaus (if identity theft is a concern)
Canada has national credit bureaus (Equifax Canada and TransUnion Canada). Fraud reporting guidance often includes checking your credit report and asking about a fraud alert.

Also tell a trusted person and your adult learning staff if you want support while you report. You are not alone — scams are common, and calm reporting is the goal.

Bookmark: antifraudcentre.ca and the CRA scam pages on canada.ca.
