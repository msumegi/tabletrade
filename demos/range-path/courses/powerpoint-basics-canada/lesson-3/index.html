Add a picture

A picture can help people remember your message. Microsoft says pictures and other visuals provide cues for the audience, but you should avoid putting too many visual aids on one slide.

To add a picture from your computer (Microsoft’s steps):

  1. On the Insert tab, select Pictures.
  2. Choose This Device (a picture saved on your computer or an internal server), browse for the picture, and choose Insert.

In PowerPoint for the web, after you insert the picture you can select it and drag to move it, and drag a corner handle to resize it. On Windows desktop, Microsoft also lists Stock Images (for Microsoft 365 subscribers) and Online Pictures. You do not need those extras for this lesson. Use a picture you have permission to use, such as a photo you took or a file your instructor gives you.

Do not copy a picture from a random website unless you know you may use it. This course is not copyright advice; when you are unsure, use your own photo or a file from class.

You can also add a simple shape. Microsoft’s steps: on the Insert tab, select Shapes, pick a shape, then click and drag on the slide to draw it. Then use the Shape Format (or Format) tab and the Shape Styles gallery if you want a colour or style.

One clear photo and a few words are enough for a community announcement slide.
