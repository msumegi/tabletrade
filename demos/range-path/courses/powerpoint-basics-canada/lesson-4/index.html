Speaker notes and a clear look

Slides work best when you do not cram in too much information. Microsoft says you can put helpful facts in speaker notes and refer to them as you present.

To add speaker notes on Windows:

  1. At the bottom of the window, click Notes.
  2. Click inside the Notes pane below the slide and type.

In PowerPoint for the web, Microsoft says to go to the View tab, select Notes, then click in the pane and type. You can drag the top border of the pane to make it larger.

Microsoft also offers these tips:

  • Choose an audience-friendly font size. Generally speaking, a font size smaller than 30 might be too difficult for the audience to see from a distance.
  • Apply a subtle, consistent theme so the background does not take attention away from your message. Built-in themes set contrast between a light background with dark text, or a dark background with light text.
  • Always check the spelling and grammar.

That font-size note is Microsoft’s general tip, not a law. Check how the slide looks from the back of the room if you can.

To change text colour or size in PowerPoint for the web, Microsoft says to select the text and use the Font options on the Home tab (including Font Color, Increase Font Size, Bold, Italic, and Underline). On Windows desktop, the basic-tasks page also describes Shape Format and WordArt tools. Look for the Home tab first.

Save as you go (Lesson 5) so you do not lose the notes you typed.
