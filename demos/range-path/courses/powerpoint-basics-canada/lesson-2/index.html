Lesson 2 of 5

Add slides and type your words

After the first slide, you add more slides for the rest of your story.

Microsoft’s steps:

  1. In the slide thumbnail pane on the left, select the slide that you want your new slide to follow.
  2. On the Home tab, select the lower half (or bottom half) of New Slide.
  3. From the menu, select the layout you want. The new slide is inserted.
  4. Click inside a placeholder and begin adding content.

A layout is the arrangement of boxes on the slide, such as a title box and a content box. Different layouts suit different slides: a title slide, a list of points, or a slide with a picture.

To add text, Microsoft says: select a text placeholder, and begin typing.

Keep the words short. Microsoft’s tips for an effective presentation say you want the audience to listen to you, instead of reading the screen. Use bullets or short sentences, and try to keep each item to one line. Microsoft also says to keep the number of slides to a minimum so the message stays clear.

A simple practise set (sample only): slide 1, “Saturday soup lunch”; slide 2, “Where and when”; slide 3, “How to help.” Type your own sample words. Do not put other people’s private information on a slide.

If the menus look a little different, look for New Slide on the Home tab and click a placeholder to type.
