Responsibilities of tenants and landlords

Even if a duty is not written in your rental agreement, Alberta.ca says landlords and tenants must still meet the RTA.

Tenants must:

  • pay rent on time
  • be considerate of the landlord and other tenants
  • not endanger other tenants
  • not perform illegal acts or conduct illegal business on the rental premises
  • keep the rental premises reasonably clean
  • prevent damage to the rental premises
  • move out when the rental agreement ends
  • follow the rules in the agreement that do not conflict with the RTA

Landlords must:

  • make the rental premises available on the date the agreement takes effect
  • give the tenant a written “notice of landlord” within 7 days of the tenant moving in, or post that notice in a visible place in a common area
  • not disturb the tenant’s peaceful enjoyment of the premises (for example, not bother the tenant beyond what is needed to do the landlord’s business)
  • keep the premises habitable at the start and throughout the tenancy (the page gives examples such as no bed bugs and working heat)

“Habitable,” on that page, means the premises meet the Minimum Housing and Health Standards under the Public Health Act and Housing Regulation.

Required notices must be delivered in person or by registered mail, unless the official page’s other service rules apply. Tenants should use the mailing address on the notice of landlord.

This list is general information from the live page, not a judgment about your building. If the premises do not feel safe or healthy, the official contact page also points tenants to Alberta Environmental Public Health. This course does not file that complaint for you.
