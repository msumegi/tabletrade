Job Bank accounts and applying online

You can browse many Job Bank postings without extra tools. If you want to save searches and get alerts, Job Bank offers free accounts.

A Standard account, according to Job Bank’s find-a-job page, can be started with an email address. With it you can customize searches, get daily Job Alerts, and use resume templates to prepare an application.

A Plus account adds more tools, such as matching you with openings from a profile, invitations from employers, and applying from some postings with a saved resume or one made in the Resume Builder.

Some extra services, including Job Match and Direct Apply, ask you to confirm that you are a Canadian citizen, a permanent resident, or a temporary resident. To prove eligibility, Job Bank may ask for your date of birth, a parent’s last name at birth, and your valid Social Insurance Number (SIN). Job Bank says the SIN is checked automatically and will not be kept in its records.

Important: If you cannot provide that information or prefer not to, Job Bank says you can still sign up by opting out of those extra services. You can add the information later if you change your mind. This course will not tell you which immigration category you are in, and you should not type your SIN into this course.

When a posting offers Direct Apply, follow the official steps on Job Bank and read any box that asks you to share your email and documents with that employer.

Keep your Job Bank password private, just as you would for email.
