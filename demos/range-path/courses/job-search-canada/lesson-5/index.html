Prepare for the interview

An interview is a meeting where an employer asks about your skills and you learn about the job. Being invited is already a step forward. This course cannot promise the job.

Job Bank says to prepare in advance:

  • Research the company.
  • Think of questions they might ask and prepare answers from your work or education.
  • Use the STAR method to structure examples: Situation, Task, Action, Result.
  • Practise with friends or family.
  • On the day, bring copies of your resume and a list of references, and dress in a way that fits the workplace.

Alis uses a similar method called STARS (Situation, Task, Action, Result, Skills) and offers four habits:

  1. Prepare — learn about the company and the job; review your resume; plan examples; prepare questions to ask them; bring a reference list. Alis also says to understand your rights about what kinds of questions are appropriate. This course is not legal advice; ask council staff or official resources if a question worries you.
  2. Practise — say answers out loud; try a practice interview with a friend.
  3. Present yourself well — be neat and on time; turn off your cellphone.
  4. Participate — listen carefully; answer thoughtfully; you may take notes. Afterward, alis suggests a short thank-you note or email that names two or three reasons you are a good fit.

If you want more help in Alberta, call the Alberta Supports Contact Centre at 1-877-644-9992, or ask your adult learning council about interview practice.
