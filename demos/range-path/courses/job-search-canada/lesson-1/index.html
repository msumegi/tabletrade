Where to look for work

A job search means looking for work in an organized way. The Government of Canada’s Job Bank is a free official website where you can browse thousands of jobs across Canada. On Job Bank you can narrow results with filters such as hours of work, benefits, and salary. You can also sign up for Job Alerts, a free email service that sends new postings, and search on the Job Bank mobile app.

Job Bank also says you can look beyond one website: attend job fairs (online or in person), contact recruiters, and use social media to grow your work network.

In Alberta, alis (Alberta’s official careers and work-search site) has a “Look for Work” section with articles on work-search basics, resumes, and how to apply. If you want to talk to someone, alis lists the Alberta Supports Contact Centre at 1-877-644-9992 (toll free).

A simple start:

  1. Open Job Bank’s find-a-job page.
  2. Type a job title or skill and a place, such as your town or a nearby city.
  3. Use filters if the list is long.
  4. Read the full posting before you apply.
  5. Write down the closing date and how to apply.

This course cannot guarantee you a job. It also will not tell you about work permits, immigration categories, or EI. Job Bank has a separate area for people outside Canada; use official pages or settlement staff for those questions.

Ask your adult learning council if they run employment-readiness workshops.
