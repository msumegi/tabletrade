Lesson 2 of 5

Type and select text

Click in the white page and start typing. Word wraps the text to the next line when you reach the end of a line, so you do not need to press Enter at the end of every line. Press Enter when you want a new paragraph.

Undo lives on the Quick Access Toolbar (Microsoft lists Undo and Redo there). If you delete something by mistake, try Undo right away.

To change text, select it first. Microsoft’s steps:

  1. Place the cursor at the beginning of the text you want to change.
  2. Press and hold the left mouse button.
  3. Drag to the right across the words. The selected text gets a coloured background.

Then you can delete the selection, type replacement words, or apply formatting (Lesson 3). To remove the selection, click once somewhere else on the page.

Practice ideas (your own words):

  • Write three short sentences about your week.
  • Write a two-line greeting, such as “Hello,” and your name.
  • Press Enter to start a new paragraph and add one more sentence.

Use Canadian spelling as you practise: centre, colour, cheque, favour. You will learn how to ask Word to check spelling in Lesson 5.

Do not type passwords, SINs, or other private numbers into a practice file on a shared computer.

If the page looks too small or too large, Microsoft’s tour includes a zoom control to change the view without changing the printed size of the words.
