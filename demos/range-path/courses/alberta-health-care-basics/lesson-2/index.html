Lesson 2 of 5

Eligibility is decided on the official application

To get a health card and be covered for insured services, you must be registered with AHCIP. The apply page says eligibility cannot be determined until your application and required documents are received and reviewed. This course cannot decide if you qualify.

The official eligibility page (as of November 2026) listed that you are eligible if you are legally entitled to be in and remain in Canada and make your permanent home in Alberta; are committed to being physically present in Alberta for at least 183 days in any 12-month period; are not claiming residency or benefits as a resident of another province, territory, or country; and are not excluded by the rules about tourists, transients, or visitors. The page also lists groups who are described as not eligible, and it has separate information for people moving to Alberta, temporary residents, and refugees. Read that live page. Do not use this lesson as immigration advice.

When you move to Alberta from another province or country, the page says there may be a waiting period before you become eligible. It does not give one number for every situation on that page, so this course does not invent one.

If coverage is denied or cancelled, the eligibility page explains how to request an eligibility review by writing to the AHCIP office. That is an official process, not something this course can do for you.

Dependants and family registration rules are also on that page. Ask a registry agent or read the live text if your family situation is complicated.
