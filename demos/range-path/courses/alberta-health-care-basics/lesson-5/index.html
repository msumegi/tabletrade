Health Link 811 and language access

Health Link 811 is Alberta’s 24-hour health-advice line. Primary Care Alberta says that for free health advice 24/7, you can call 811 and speak with a registered nurse. Alberta’s Know Your Options page says to call 811 if you are unsure where to go, for advice on your symptoms and care options.

The newcomers page also says you can call 811 24 hours a day, 365 days a year to receive health advice in your language. Say the language you need when the call is answered, and a medical interpreter will be added to translate.

811 is advice and navigation. It is not the same as calling 911 for a medical emergency. If you are not sure which number to use, the official “unsure where to go” instruction is to call 811 — unless it is an emergency, in which case call 911.

MyHealth Alberta’s numbers page also lists other lines, including 211 for community and social services and the Poison and Drug Information Service (PADIS) at 1-800-332-1414. Use those official listings; this course is not a poison-control class.

If you want information in writing, Primary Care Alberta notes that some health-system resources are translated into languages commonly used in Alberta.

Keep your health card and photo ID together. Register or update your AHCIP information at a registry agent, not by emailing this course.
