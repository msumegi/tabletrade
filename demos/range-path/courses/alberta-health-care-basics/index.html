Alberta Health Care Basics (AHCIP, 811, and Finding a Provider)

By the end, learners can describe what AHCIP is, point to the official eligibility and application pages without deciding their own coverage, explain how to apply through a registry agent, show a health card with photo identification, find providers on Alberta Find a Provider, and choose 911 for a medical emergency and 811 for 24/7 health advice.
