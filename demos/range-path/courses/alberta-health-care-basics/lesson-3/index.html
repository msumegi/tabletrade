How to apply

All new and returning eligible Alberta residents must register for AHCIP to receive a personal health card and insured hospital and health services.

As of November 2026, the “How to apply” page listed these steps:

  1. Read the eligibility requirements.
  2. Complete the Application form for AHCIP Coverage (forms are also available from a registry agent). There is a different form for some Canadian children of parents who are not eligible — use it only if the page says it fits your situation.
  3. Gather original supporting documents that prove Alberta residency, identity, and legal entitlement to be in and remain in Canada. The page lists examples such as an Alberta driver’s licence or a rental agreement for residency, and a passport or other government identity documents. If required documents are missing, the application cannot be processed.
  4. Bring the completed form and your original documents to a registry agent. The page said there is no charge for this service. Processing time can take up to 5 days. Paper cards are mailed to your home, so give a current mailing address.

Coverage begins once your application has been approved and you are registered. If you need care before you have your card, you may be asked to pay or be billed if you do not have other insurance. You will not be refused emergency care.

When you use services, you will need to show your personal health card (paper or mobile) along with photo identification.

This course does not fill in the form or choose documents for you.
