Lesson 5 of 5

Do the work, check it, and close

Implementation, in the TBS glossary, is directing, managing, performing, and accomplishing the project work; providing the deliverables; and providing work performance information (in plain words: doing the tasks and telling people how they are going).

Skills for Success collaboration includes taking responsibility for your part, sharing information when needed, and letting others contribute. Problem solving includes watching whether a choice is working and learning from the result.

A simple rhythm:

  • Do the next task on the list.
  • Mark it done.
  • If something blocks you, say so early.
  • Compare progress with the dates you wrote.

Close-out is the final phase. TBS says it involves final acceptance of the deliverables, archiving the project documents, and disbanding the project team. It also happens if a project ends early. For a small project that can mean: walk through the result with the person who asked for it, write a short note about what was finished and what was learned, file the receipts or lists, and thank the people who helped.

Acceptance means the agreed work is done — not that every new idea anyone just thought of is included. New ideas can become a new project.
