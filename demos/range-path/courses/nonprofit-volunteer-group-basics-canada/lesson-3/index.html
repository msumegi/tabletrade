The official map for incorporating a society

Alberta publishes the steps to incorporate a society. This lesson is a map, not a do-it-yourself legal kit. Always use the live page and the current forms.

As of 10 November 2026 (America/Regina), the incorporation fee was $53.05. The page said to make your cheque payable to “Government of Alberta” and not to send a credit card number in the mail.

Step 1 — Choose a name. A society’s name has three parts: a distinctive element (something unique, often a place or word), a descriptive element (what the society does), and a legal element. Accepted legal elements include Society, Association, Club, Fellowship, Guild, Foundation, Institute, League, Committee, Council, Board, Centre, and Bureau. Alberta’s example is “Smith Falls White Water Rafting Association.”

Step 2 — Get an Alberta NUANS report. Review it to make sure no other society has an identical name. Identical names are not allowed. The report reserves the proposed name for 90 days and must be submitted with the incorporation details while it is still valid.

Step 3 — Fill out the forms named on the page, including an application to form a society, bylaws, and notices of address and directors.

Step 4 — Submit signed duplicates of the completed forms, the NUANS report, and the fee to Corporate Registry at the address on the live page.

If the information meets requirements, Corporate Registry mails a certificate of incorporation. You will receive an email when a federal business number is issued.

This course does not write your bylaws or choose your name. Fees and addresses can change — check the live page before you send anything.
