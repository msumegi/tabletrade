Plan before you go

Rural Alberta winters can bring cold, wind, ice, and sudden storms. Alberta’s safer-winter-highways guidance says to stay off the road during winter storms unless travel is necessary. If roads are closed, do not travel.

Before a rural trip:

  1. Check road and weather conditions — use official tools such as 511 Alberta (511.alberta.ca) and weather forecasts.
  2. Tell someone your route, destination, and when you expect to arrive (AMA rural advice stresses this).
  3. Ask: Can this wait until conditions improve?
  4. Charge your phone and pack a charger or power bank.
  5. Know that some foothills and valley areas can have weak GPS or mobile signal — AMA notes carrying a paper map as backup and watching road markers and landmarks.

Pack patience. Arriving late is better than not arriving. Your adult learning council may also share local tips (for example, which highways drift first) — ask staff.

This course does not replace checking today’s conditions. Make “check 511” a habit every winter morning you plan to drive.
