Lesson 5 of 5

If you become stranded or need help

Injuries or immediate danger: Call 911.

Unharmed but need a tow / roadside help: AMA members can call AMA; others should use their roadside plan or local towing. Give clear location details — road markers, nearby towns, landmarks — because GPS pins can fail.

If you stay with the vehicle (common advice when it is safer than walking in a storm):

  • Run the engine only as needed for heat.
  • Keep the tailpipe clear of snow so carbon monoxide does not enter the cabin (AMA rural advice).
  • Open a window slightly for fresh air when the engine runs.
  • Use blankets and spare clothes.
  • Make the vehicle visible with lights or markers if safe.

Do not walk away from the vehicle in a blizzard unless you can see a safe building very close. Walking in deep cold and wind can be deadly.

After you are safe, tell the person who had your trip plan that you arrived or that you needed help. Review what to add to your kit before the next trip.

Remember: staying home is a valid safety choice.
