Winter Safety & Driving in Rural Alberta

Plan a rural winter trip, pack a kit, and know safer habits for ice, snow, and getting stranded.

Learners can prepare a vehicle and trip plan for rural winter travel, list emergency-kit basics, describe safer speed and following-distance habits, recognise ice and low-visibility risks, and know when to stay home or call for help.

This is not driver training. It is general safety information. It does not replace the Alberta Driver’s Guide or a qualified instructor.
