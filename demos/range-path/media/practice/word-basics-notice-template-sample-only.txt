Word Basics (Canada) — one-page community notice
Sample only. Draft dated 10 November 2026 (America/Regina).
We wrote this notice ourselves. It is not a real event and is not copied from another design.

Saturday market at the community centre

Please join neighbours at the Sampletown community centre this Saturday from 10 a.m. to 1 p.m.

Bring:
- Your own bags
- Small bills or a cheque if you plan to buy baking
- A colourful reusable cup if you want tea

This practice notice is sample text only. Ask a tutor before you print anything for a real posting. Use a Heading 1 style on the title, a short list for the “Bring” items, and Canadian spelling (centre, cheque, colourful).
