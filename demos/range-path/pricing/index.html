Questions

Who pays?

The council (or a municipal or FCSS host) pays CA$149 per month. Learners pay nothing.

Is this accredited?

No. A certificate confirms that someone finished a course. It is not a college diploma, degree, trade ticket, or professional licence.

What does the certificate say?

It names the learner and the course, gives the date, and states: “This certificate confirms course completion only and is not an accredited credential.” It also says it is not a diploma, degree, trade ticket, professional licence, or immigration, legal, tax, or financial advice. The certificate opens on this device only after a final quiz of 70% or more.

Do you store learner personal data?

In this demo, no account exists. A name for the certificate and quiz progress stay in the browser on that device. The demo does not process personal information for a real council.

Is this legal, tax, or immigration advice?

No. Courses are general information with links to Canada.ca and Alberta.ca. Ask council staff about your own situation.

Accessibility?

Aimed at WCAG AA. Plain language, large type, and big buttons. Short sentences, with official terms explained once.

Which devices?

Phone browsers first (iPhone Safari and Android Chrome). It also works on a tablet or a desktop computer. No app to install.
