Delete your data

TableTrade is an Android app from Range Road Technologies (com.rangeroadtech.tabletrade). Your lists and photo stay on this phone. We do not keep a copy on a Range Road server.

Last updated 12 November 2026. This page describes the app as built. It is not legal advice. Privacy.

On this phone

There is no account and no password. These are the controls the app actually has.

  1. Remove a card. Open Have or Want. On a card, tap the × button (it is named Remove, then the card name). That card leaves that list.
  2. Clear the face photo. Open You. If a photo is there, tap Remove. Change photo or Add a photo replaces it. The photo is not sent while you are looking.
  3. Clear your name and note. On You, erase Display name and I’m over here. If the name is blank the next time the app opens, it shows Trainer. The random id the app made for this phone stays until you clear app data or uninstall.
  4. Stop sharing. On Nearby, tap Done looking. This phone stops offering your name and lists over Bluetooth.
  5. Hide someone on this phone. On a ping, tap Don’t show this person. Their id stays on this phone so you do not see them again. There is no Unblock button. Hear pings again clears the recent match ids, not that block list.

To wipe everything the app saved on this phone, uninstall TableTrade, or use Android Settings > Apps > TableTrade > Storage > Clear data. Android backup is turned off for this app, so a backup does not keep a second copy.

Email us

Write to help@rangeroadtech.com if you want us to delete a message you already sent, or if you have a question. Email does not delete the lists on your phone. Use the steps above for that.

In the email, include:

  • That you want TableTrade data deleted.
  • The display name you used in the app.
  • The address you are writing from, and any earlier message we should find.

We keep those emails only as long as we need them to answer. The app does not set a mailbox timer. We aim to reply within 30 days.

What is deleted

Clear data or uninstall removes what this phone saved for TableTrade:

  • Have list and want list.
  • Display name, the I’m over here note (up to 40 characters), and the random user id.
  • The optional face photo. It is a small picture stored only on this phone.
  • The yes or no that you are 13 or older.
  • Ids of people you chose with Don’t show this person.
  • The last 80 match ids, so the same ping is not shown again.
  • The last 80 card-name searches saved on the phone so a search can be shown again without the network.

Removing one card does not clear the photo, the name, or the search cache. Remove on You clears only the photo.

What we do not have

Range Road Technologies does not keep a server copy of your lists, photo, name, or looking data. There is no account to delete. The app does not use analytics, ads, or a crash reporter.

Card search sends the name you type, or the name read from a card on the phone, to the Pokémon TCG API at api.pokemontcg.io. Card pictures load from images.pokemontcg.io. That is their service, not ours. We cannot delete their logs. Scanning a card uses the camera on the phone. Those frames are not saved as photos. The scan tool may download its language files from cdn.jsdelivr.net and tessdata.projectnaptha.com. Your lists and photo are not uploaded there.

What other phones already have

While you are looking, Bluetooth (not the internet) can share this with other TableTrade phones in the room: your user id, display name (up to 24 characters), the I’m over here note if you wrote one, have and want card ids (up to 12 each), and a time. The face photo is not included.

We cannot recall that from their phones. Done looking stops this phone from sharing more. A phone that already received it keeps the name, note, and card ids only in memory, and only while TableTrade stays open (the newest 20 matches on screen). The Bluetooth path has no timer. Closing TableTrade on that phone drops the name, note, and full lists.

That phone may still keep a shorter match id: your user id, the kind of match, and the card ids that overlapped. It keeps the last 80 of those ids until they tap Hear pings again, clear TableTrade’s data, or uninstall. It does not keep your photo.
