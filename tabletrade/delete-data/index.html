Delete your data

TableTrade is an Android app from Range Road Technologies (com.rangeroadtech.tabletrade). Your lists and photo are saved only on your phone. We do not keep a copy on a Range Road server. When you tap I’m looking, your name, note and card lists are shared over encrypted Bluetooth with phones nearby. Your photo is never shared.

Last updated 12 November 2026. This page describes the app as built. It is not legal advice. Privacy.

On this phone

There is no account and no password. These are the controls the app actually has.

  1. Remove a card. Open Have or Want. On a card, tap the × in the card’s corner. That card leaves that list.
  2. Clear the face photo. Open You. If a photo is there, tap Remove. To use a different photo, tap Change photo. The photo is not sent while you are looking.
  3. Clear your name and note. On You, erase Display name and I’m over here. If the name is blank the next time the app opens, it shows Trainer. The random ID the app made for this phone stays until you clear app data or uninstall.
  4. Stop sharing. On Nearby, tap Done looking. This phone stops offering your name and lists over Bluetooth. Closing TableTrade also stops it. Switching to another app may not.
  5. Hide someone on this phone. On a ping, tap Don’t show this person. Their ID stays on this phone so you do not see them again. There is no Unblock button. Hear pings again (on Nearby, under your pings; it appears once a ping is on screen) clears the recent match codes, not that block list.

To wipe everything the app saved on this phone, uninstall TableTrade, or use Android Settings > Apps > TableTrade > Storage > Clear data. Android cloud backup is off for this app. On some phones, a direct phone-to-phone transfer may still copy TableTrade’s data to a new phone.

Email us

Write to help@rangeroadtech.com if you want us to delete a message you already sent, or if you have a question. Email does not delete the lists on your phone. Use the steps above for that.

In the email, include:

  • That you want TableTrade data deleted.
  • The address you are writing from, and any earlier message we should find.

We aim to reply within 30 days. We delete your email and our reply within 90 days after we answer.

What is deleted

Clear data or uninstall removes everything TableTrade saved on this phone, including:

  • Have list and want list.
  • Display name, the I’m over here note (up to 40 characters), and the random user ID.
  • The optional face photo. It is a small picture stored only on this phone.
  • The yes or no that you are 13 or older.
  • IDs of people you chose with Don’t show this person.
  • The last 80 match codes, so the same ping is not shown again.
  • The last 80 card-name searches saved on the phone so a search can be shown again without the network.
  • Saved card pictures and scan files.

Removing one card does not clear the photo, the name, or the search cache. Remove on You clears only the photo.

What we do not have

Range Road Technologies does not keep a server copy of your lists, photo, name, or looking data. There is no account to delete. The app does not use analytics, ads, or a crash reporter.

Card search sends the name you type, or the name read from a card on the phone, to the Pokémon TCG API at api.pokemontcg.io. Card pictures load from images.pokemontcg.io. That is their service, not ours. We cannot delete their logs. Scanning a card uses the camera on the phone. Those frames are not saved as photos. The scan tool may download its language files from cdn.jsdelivr.net and tessdata.projectnaptha.com. Your lists and photo are not uploaded there. The app loads its fonts from Google when it starts, which sends your phone’s IP address. No lists or photos.

What other phones already have

While you are looking, your phone offers this over encrypted Bluetooth to phones nearby (usually the same room, sometimes a little farther). A device that only listens cannot read it. Any phone running TableTrade nearby can, because that is how matching works, and so could a tool built to talk like TableTrade. The Bluetooth signal itself still shows that a TableTrade phone is nearby. What is offered: your user ID, display name (up to 24 characters), the I’m over here note if you wrote one, have and want card IDs (up to 12 each), and a time. The face photo is not included.

We cannot recall that from their phones. Done looking stops this phone from sharing more. A phone that already received it keeps the name, note, and card IDs only in memory, and only while TableTrade stays open (the newest 20 matches on screen). Closing TableTrade on that phone drops the name, note, and full lists.

That phone also saves a short match code so it does not ping you twice: your random TableTrade ID, the kind of match, and the card IDs that matched. It does not save your name, note, or photo. It keeps its last 80 codes until that person taps Hear pings again (on Nearby, once a ping is on screen), clears TableTrade’s data, or uninstalls. If they tapped Don’t show this person, your ID stays on their phone until they clear TableTrade’s data.
