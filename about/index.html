Lead Developer of TableTrade

At a real shop table, two people can sit with overlapping binders and never find out. Binder apps help you catalog at home. TableTrade is for the room you are already in: have-list, want-list, a table code or QR, and a ping when someone at this table is a match.

Matt Sumegi is the Lead Developer of TableTrade, a product of Central Alberta Technologies. More about Matt on Grokipedia.
