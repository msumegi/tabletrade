How it works

  1. Open it

    Browser or home-screen app. You are already at the shop.

  2. Load the lists

    Add a few haves and wants. Scan one card at a time, or search a name and tap the printing.

  3. Join the table

    One phone shows a table code or QR. The other types it or scans. That is the join.

  4. The ping

    If the lists overlap, both phones fire. You are already here — trade now.

FAQ

Do I need an account?
No. Lists live on the phone. No password.
How do people join a table?
A four-character table code or a QR from someone already in — that is the real join. Optional “I’m at the shop” GPS is about a room away (~120 m) and often fails indoors or in a basement. Use the code or QR.
iPhone — Add to Home Screen
Open the app in Safari → tap Share → Add to Home Screen. Then open TableTrade like any other app.
Android Chrome — install
Open the app in Chrome → tap the menu (⋮) → Add to Home screen or Install app.
What is private?
Have-lists and want-lists stay on the device until you join a table. At the table, your display name and the cards on those lists are what get matched. Camera is for scanning a card or another player’s QR. Location is optional and is not stored as the product. Short privacy note.
Is this official?
No. TableTrade is an unofficial fan tool. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Nintendo or The Pokémon Company.
